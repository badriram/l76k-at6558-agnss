# Heltec L76K, 2026-10-17

**Hardware**: Heltec L76K GNSS module (the plug-in for the WiFi LoRa 32 V4 and Mesh Node T114),
on a Heltec WiFi LoRa 32 V4 (ESP32-S3) running MicroPython v1.29.0. The receiver reports
`IC=AT6558R-5N-32-1C580901`, `SW=URANUS5,V5.3.0.0`, `TB=2020-03-26`, mode GPS + BeiDou. The V4
switches the module's main supply, so a "power cut" below removes the main supply while the
module's backup supply stays up.

**Conditions**: outdoors, clear sunny afternoon, residential city street, antenna facing up, board
still. 5 to 17 satellites in use. Times are from the receiver's boot or restart to the first GGA
with a fix and at least four satellites.

## Cold, hot and standby

| case | runs (s) | notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| cold restart, `$PCAS10,2`, no help | 34.7, 44.8, 40.0, 33.5, 39.0, 42.3, 42.3 | ~40 s |
| module unplugged 2 minutes (no backup), first fix | 36.2 | a true cold start |
| main supply cut 60 s, backup kept | 1.6, 1.9, 1.7 | hot start; boots in 0.03-0.19 s |
| STANDBY pin low 60 s, then high | 2.3, 1.1, 2.1 | output resumes in 0.27 s |
| `$PCAS10,1` "warm" restart | 2.7, 2.7, 1.5 | keeps the orbit data: a hot start in effect |
| `$PCAS12,<s>` timed standby | — | not supported: output never stopped |

## Assisted cold starts

Each run: `$PCAS10,2`, wait for a GGA reporting no fix (the restart confirmed), wait one second,
inject, and time from the restart. Four cases per round, a different order each round. Orbit data:
CASIC's AGNSS file fetched minutes before (32 MSG-GPSEPH, 1 MSG-GPSION, 2,650 bytes, GPS only).

| case | round 1 | round 2 | round 3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| no help | 39.0 | 42.3 | 42.3 |
| AID-INI (time, position) | 33.2 | 38.2 | 38.2 |
| orbit data | 30.3 | 26.3 | 34.4 |
| **AID-INI + orbit data** | **6.3** | **6.3** | **5.3** |

Acknowledgements: AID-INI acknowledged first time in every run; all 32 MSG-GPSEPH and the
MSG-GPSION acknowledged in every run (indoors earlier, 27 of 32, 5 refused).

## Earlier, and what did not work

- AID-INI sent within ~0.05 s of a restart was not acknowledged (8 of 9 runs) and made no
  difference: wait about a second.
- Polling the receiver's own orbit data (`CFG-MSG` for 0x08 0x07 / 0x06 / 0x05 at rate 0xFFFF,
  and at rate 1), 42 times across runs: acknowledged, never answered. No capture and replay.
- A wrong-position check (AID-INI 10° of latitude off, claimed accurate to 10 m) was run only
  while injections were being ignored, plus one valid run (30.7 s): not conclusive.

## Not measured

Fixes after hours off (orbit data expired); currents; BeiDou assistance.
